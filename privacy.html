Privacy

Privacy, in plain words

What this site collects, who sees it, and how to ask me to change or remove anything.

Last updated 2 November 2026

Who runs this site

is a small, remote publication run by (“I”). If you have any question about this page, email me.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking scripts on this site.
  • The site does not set cookies.
  • I only receive personal information if you choose to send it to me, for example through the contact form, by email, on WhatsApp or when you book a call.

The contact form

If you use “Send a message”, I receive your name, your email address, the business name or link if you add one, and your message. I use them only to reply to you and to talk about being featured or about website work. The message is delivered to my inbox by a third-party form service (FormSubmit), which handles it on its way to me. If the form cannot send, your own email app opens with the message ready, and then it is an ordinary email between you and me.

I keep messages for as long as we are talking or working together, and delete them when you ask me to.

Nominations and the newsletter

If you nominate a founder, I receive your name and email and the details you give about them, and I use them only to follow up on that nomination. I contact the founder only to invite them, and nothing about them is published without their written approval. If you subscribe to the newsletter, I receive your email address and use it only to send the roundup. You can unsubscribe any time by replying to any email, and I will remove you.

Email, WhatsApp, Instagram and Calendly

The icons and buttons on this site open other services: your email app, WhatsApp, Instagram and Calendly. Those services have their own privacy policies and collect their own data once you open them. What you share with me there, such as your name, number, handle or the time you book, I use only to reply to you and arrange our chat.

Fonts

The site’s fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers when a page opens, and Google can see your IP address as part of that request.

Listen, share cards and videos

The “Listen to this story” button uses your browser’s own text-to-speech; depending on your browser, the voice may be provided by your device or by your browser’s maker. Quote cards are drawn on your own device and are not sent anywhere. If a story includes a YouTube video, it loads from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain, and YouTube can see your IP address when it does.

What is stored in your browser

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser so the site remembers it next time. It stays on your device and is not sent to me or to anyone else. You can clear it any time in your browser settings.

Stories about founders

The stories on this site are published only with the founder’s written approval. A story can include the founder’s name, business name, town and country, the year they started, their Instagram handle and photos they approved. If you are featured and want something corrected, changed or removed, email me and I will do it promptly.

Stories marked “Sample” are examples written for this site.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, the UK GDPR, or India’s data protection law), you may have the right to ask what information I hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Email me and I will answer as soon as I can.

Changes to this page

If I add anything new, such as analytics, I will update this page first. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
