    About

    Hello, I'm .

    Most of the founders I come across have a story worth telling, and almost none of them have the time to tell it. They're teaching, answering messages and washing the blankets. So I started to do that part for them.

    I'm a web designer, and I work remotely, so we talk by video at a time that suits you. I ask how it began, what the first year was like, and what a normal week looks like. Then I write it up in plain, warm language, the way you might tell it to a friend.

    Nothing is published until you've read the draft and said yes in writing. You can change anything, or ask me to take your story down later. There is no fee, and no catch.

    I also build websites, and there's a short section on that further down for anyone who ever wants one. It's separate from the stories, and being featured never depends on it.

    Our mission

    Why exists.

    is new and still small. That is exactly why it is worth saying clearly what it is for, who it is for, and where it is going.

    The goal

    To make sure the independent people behind yoga and Pilates studios, martial arts schools and fitness classes get a story that is told well and can be found. Most of them are too busy teaching to write about themselves, so I do the writing. The founder approves every word in writing, and the story is published free on its own page that search engines and other people can find.

    Who it is for

    Founders: independent yoga teachers, Pilates instructors, studio owners, and martial arts and fitness instructors, anywhere in the world, especially people running something small on their own.

    Readers: students looking for a teacher or studio with a real story behind it, and anyone curious about what it takes to start something small and personal.

    Where it is heading

    A growing library of real, approved stories from many countries. It starts with yoga and Pilates, and grows into martial arts and fitness. Each story gets its own page, a shareable quote card and a badge the founder can use.

    The stories stay free for founders. I also design websites for founders who want one. That is a separate service, and being featured never depends on it.

    Right now the reach is small, and I would rather build this slowly and well than loudly.
